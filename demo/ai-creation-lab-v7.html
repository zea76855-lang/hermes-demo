Core Capabilities

AI-Powered Full-Stack

From image recognition to 3D generation, from DFM validation to instant quoting — product development has never been this efficient.

Image to 3D
Upload photos from any angle. AI generates accurate 3D models with multi-view fusion recognition.
Text to 3D
Describe your product in natural language. AI understands intent and generates editable 3D models.
Instant Quotation
Upload drawings, AI analyzes manufacturing paths and generates real-time quotes for CNC, injection molding, and 3D printing.

One-Click Order
